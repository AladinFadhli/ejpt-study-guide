# Resources

## Official

- [eJPT certification page](https://ine.com/security/certifications/ejpt-certification) — authoritative on current objectives
- **INE Penetration Testing Student (PTS) learning path** — the official associated course
- Always confirm current exam format and objectives on INE's site; they revise periodically (the exam was refreshed in 2026)

## Free practice (authorised targets)

- [TryHackMe](https://tryhackme.com/) — guided, beginner-friendly; the best starting point for methodology
- [Hack The Box](https://www.hackthebox.com/) — realistic boot-to-root machines; beginner tracks available
- [PortSwigger Web Security Academy](https://portswigger.net/web-security) — free, excellent web security labs
- [VulnHub](https://www.vulnhub.com/) — downloadable vulnerable VMs for your own lab

## Reference

- [Nmap documentation](https://nmap.org/book/man.html)

- [PayloadsAllTheThings](https://github.com/swisskyrepo/PayloadsAllTheThings) — technique reference (use responsibly, in-scope only)

## Tooling

- **Kali Linux** or **Parrot OS** — attacker distributions with tools preinstalled
- **VirtualBox** (free) or **VMware** — hypervisors for your lab

## On ethics and legality

Every resource here is for use in your own lab or on platforms that authorise testing. Read [ETHICS.md](ETHICS.md). Only test what you own or are permitted to test — a certification is not permission.

## A note on exam integrity

The eJPT is under NDA. Do not seek or share actual exam tasks, flags, or walkthroughs of the real exam — it violates the certification agreement and can revoke your cert. Learn the methodology, which is what this repo provides, and the exam takes care of itself.
