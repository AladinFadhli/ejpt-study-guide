# Building a Practice Lab

The eJPT is hands-on, so you learn it by doing — legally, in an environment you own or are explicitly authorised to use. This folder explains how to set that up.

> **The rule:** only ever attack systems you own or have written permission to test. A home lab and authorised platforms give you unlimited legal practice. See [ETHICS.md](../ETHICS.md).

## Contents

- [Home lab setup](home-lab-setup.md) — a self-contained lab on your own machine
- [Practice targets](practice-targets.md) — where to get authorised, legal targets

## Why a lab matters

Reading about enumeration and privilege escalation teaches you the concepts. Only repetition makes them automatic — and on a 48-hour practical exam, automatic is what you need. Every technique in the [methodology](../methodology/) should be something you've *done*, not just read.

Aim to reach the point where the workflow — scan, enumerate every service, identify the weakness, exploit, escalate, pivot — is muscle memory. That only comes from reps.
