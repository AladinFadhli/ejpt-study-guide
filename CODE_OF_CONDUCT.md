# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project is aimed largely at people who are new to cloud computing, and often new to open source. Assume every question is asked in good faith by someone doing their best.

**Expected:**
- Being welcoming and patient, especially with beginners
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for people trying to learn
- Correcting factual errors kindly and with a source

**Not acceptable:**
- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or imagery of any kind
- Dismissiveness toward people asking basic questions
- Publishing others' private information
- Encouraging people to use exam dumps

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and any space where someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report problems by opening an issue marked `conduct` or contacting the maintainers privately. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they consider inappropriate.
