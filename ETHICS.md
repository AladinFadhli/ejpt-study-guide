# Ethics and Legal Use

This repository is study material for a professional penetration testing / defensive security certification. Everything here is written to help people **learn the methodology and pass the exam**, and to do the job **legally and professionally**.

## The one rule that matters

**Only test systems you own or have explicit, written permission to test.**

Unauthorised access to computer systems is a crime almost everywhere — the Computer Fraud and Abuse Act (US), the Computer Misuse Act (UK), and equivalent laws worldwide carry serious criminal penalties. A certification does not grant permission. An engagement contract, a signed scope, or your own lab does.

## What this repo is

- Methodology: how to approach each phase of an assessment
- Concepts: what each class of vulnerability is and why it works
- Tool reference: what standard, publicly-documented tools do and when to use them
- Lab guidance: how to build a **legal, isolated practice environment** you own
- Reporting: how to communicate findings professionally

## What this repo is not

- It is not a collection of working exploits or copy-paste attack payloads
- It does not target real organisations, live systems, or anyone's infrastructure
- It is not a shortcut around actually learning the material

Where a technique is described, it is described at the level needed to understand and defend against it and to reproduce it **in your own lab** — not packaged for use against systems you don't control.

## Practice legally

Build a lab (see the `labs/` folder). Use intentionally vulnerable targets designed for this purpose, and platforms that provide authorised targets such as Hack The Box and TryHackMe. These give you unlimited legal reps.

## Professional conduct

Penetration testing is a trust profession. Clients grant access to their most sensitive systems on the understanding that you will act ethically, stay in scope, protect what you find, and report honestly. The technical skills are half the job; the professionalism is the other half, and it's what makes a career sustainable.

If you're using these skills at work, follow your rules of engagement, your scope, and your disclosure obligations without exception.
